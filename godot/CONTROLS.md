# Kindling - Controls Guide

## Player 1 (Keyboard)

### Movement
- **Left**: A
- **Right**: D  
- **Up**: W
- **Down**: S

### Actions
- **Jump**: Space
  - Press again in air for double jump
  - Hold Down while falling for fast fall
- **Attack**: J
- **Special**: K

### Techniques
- **Walk**: Light tap on movement keys
- **Run**: Hold movement keys
- **Fast Fall**: Press Down while airborne and falling

## Player 2 (Keyboard or Controller)

### Movement (Keyboard)
- **Left**: Left Arrow
- **Right**: Right Arrow
- **Up**: Up Arrow  
- **Down**: Down Arrow

### Movement (Controller)
- **Movement**: Left Analog Stick or D-Pad

### Actions (Keyboard)
- **Jump**: Numpad 0
  - Press again in air for double jump
  - Hold Down while falling for fast fall
- **Attack**: Numpad 1
- **Special**: Numpad 2

### Actions (Controller)
- **Jump**: A Button (South)
  - Press again in air for double jump
  - Hold Down while falling for fast fall
- **Attack**: X Button (West)
- **Special**: Y Button (North)

### Techniques
- **Walk**: Light analog stick input
- **Run**: Full analog stick input
- **Fast Fall**: Press Down while airborne and falling

## Game Mechanics

### Core Health System
- Each player has a two-tiered Core health bar
- **Outer Core** (Orange): 50 HP
- **Inner Core** (Red/Ember): 50 HP
- When Outer Core depletes, Inner Core is exposed
- **Inner Core Exposed = 1.5x Knockback Multiplier**

### Kindle System
- Special meter that fills as you fight
- Gain Kindle by:
  - Landing attacks
  - Taking damage
- Spend Kindle on special moves
- Decays slowly after 3 seconds of inactivity

### Knockback & Victory
- Damage increases knockback distance
- Higher damage % = fly further when hit
- **Ring-out** (Ignition): Get launched beyond blast zones to lose
- Strategic use of Inner Core multiplier creates comebacks

### Damage Display
- Shows total damage percentage
- Higher % = more vulnerable to ring-outs
- Affects knockback calculation

## Tips & Strategy

1. **Manage Your Core**: Breaking through to Inner Core makes you vulnerable
2. **Build Kindle**: Save it for critical moments or recovery
3. **Knockback Awareness**: High damage + Inner Core exposed = danger zone
4. **Stage Control**: Stay near center, force opponent to edges
5. **Combo Potential**: Hitstun allows for follow-up attacks

## Fire-Themed Terminology

- **Launch**: Knockback
- **Ignition**: KO/Ring-out
- **Stoking**: Charging
- **Kindle**: Special meter
- **Core**: Health (Outer/Inner layers)
